# Squat Form Guide

## Setup
- Feet roughly shoulder-width, toes slightly out.
- Brace the core before every rep (big breath into the belly, ribs down).
- Eyes forward; keep the chest proud without excessive lumbar arch.

## Descent
- Sit the hips back and down together — do not dive the knees first.
- Knees track over the toes; avoid collapsing inward.
- Aim for thighs at least parallel to the floor if mobility and knee comfort allow.

## Ascent
- Drive through mid-foot / heel.
- Keep the bar path over mid-foot.
- Lock out hips and knees together; do not hyperextend the lower back.

## Common faults
- Knees caving → push knees out, cue "screw feet into the floor".
- Rounding lower back at depth → raise depth, brace harder, or reduce load.
- Heels lifting → widen stance slightly or improve ankle mobility.

## Knee-friendly cues
- Prefer controlled tempo over bouncing out of the hole.
- If the left knee is irritated, shorten depth slightly and keep load moderate.
- Stop if sharp joint pain appears (dull muscle burn is OK).
