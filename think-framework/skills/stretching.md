# Stretching & Mobility Guide

## When to stretch
- Before training: dynamic mobility (2–5 min), not long static holds.
- After training: light static stretches 20–40 s per area.

## Dynamic warm-up (pre-workout)
- Leg swings (front/back and side).
- World's greatest stretch / hip openers.
- Bodyweight squats and lunges.
- Ankle circles and calf rocks.

## Post-workout focus areas
- Hip flexors and quads after squats / running.
- Hamstrings and glutes after hinge or hills.
- Calves and ankles after running.
- Thoracic openers if upper back feels stiff.

## Knee-friendly mobility
- Prefer pain-free ranges only.
- Quad stretch without forcing end range if the knee complains.
- Add gentle terminal knee extensions and sit-to-stand control work.

## Rules
- Never bounce into a stretch.
- Breathe steadily; stop on sharp pain.
- Consistency beats intensity — short daily mobility beats rare long sessions.
